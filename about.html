Why Jenny Consultings

Most agencies work one side of the table. We've sat on both — recruiting inside healthcare and technology organizations across Nigeria, Canada, and the USA — so we know what a good hire actually looks like from the inside, not just from a job description.

That means less time wasted on candidates who look right on paper but aren't, and less time spent by candidates chasing roles that were never going to be a fit.

Built for both sides

For employers

You get candidates who are already vetted for the realities of the role — licensing, shift patterns, technical depth — not just a résumé that matches keywords.

For candidates
